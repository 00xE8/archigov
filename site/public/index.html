How It Works

Four ideas, enforced end to end. The product never asserts compliance — it shows evidence and who stands behind it.

Derived, Not Drawn

Azure Resource Graph, the delivery backlog and your existing repositories populate the as-is model with provenance. Humans spend their effort on decisions, standards and reviews — never on keeping diagrams honest. Deterministic ArchiMate views come free.

Machine-Checkable Governance

Standards, principles, patterns and policies are human statements with control references and, where possible, a checkable rule. Every adopted rule is evaluated continuously; resource-level checks arrive as imported evidence from Defender for Cloud and Azure Policy, never rebuilt.

Boards Decide, on the Record

Typed reviews route to boards with areas of responsibility. Decision makers record their own positions, the chair declares what the positions allow, sessions derive their minutes from the records — and all of it lands in a SHA-256 audit chain.

Signed Evidence, Continuously

Findings persist across runs, exceptions and conditions are tracked to resolution, and the evidence pack is Ed25519-signed against the chain head. OSCAL and DORA exports carry the same discipline — and the same disclaimer.

AI Proposes. Humans Decide.

Eight AI features, each enabled separately by your admin with its own provider and model — OpenAI, Claude, Mistral or a local Ollama. Every one keeps a deterministic fallback, every output is marked AI-generated and unverified, and nothing an AI writes ever approves, blocks or becomes the record without a named human's audited act.

  • Pre-review — cited findings on a design; citations must resolve or they are discarded
  • Decision review — a critical test of the argument: unsupported claims, unstated risks, contradictions with what you already adopted
  • Rule drafting — natural language to a checkable rule, validated and dry-run before anyone saves it
  • Evidence suggestions — missing evidence becomes a tracked assessment in one click

Open Source, Without the Rug-Pull

The core is AGPL-3.0. Run it yourself — even modified — for your own organisation with no obligations. Self-host from one container with SQLite inside; continuous Litestream replication if you want it. A managed UK/EU instance is available for teams that would rather not run it — reach out on GitHub.

Get the code

The commitment, made on day one: this project will never move to a licence more restrictive than AGPL-3.0. No BSL, no SSPL. Connectors, templates and the policy SDK will be published under Apache-2.0 as they split out, so integrating with Archigov never requires touching AGPL code.
